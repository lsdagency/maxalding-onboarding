# Edit QA checklist

Two halves. The machine half is `python -m editor.cli qa`; it must show zero errors. The eye half is done on the contact sheet, the first frame and the timeline transcript before any cut goes to Liam.

## Machine (editor/qa.py)
- Frame size, frame rate, codec, audio format, file name.
- Loudness -14 LUFS plus or minus 1.5, true peak under -1 dBTP, audio not silent.
- No black opening frame; black frames and dead air mid-ad flagged.
- Every text event inside the safe zone, at least 54 px, captions two lines or fewer, no caption flashing under 0.25 s.
- Every on-screen word through the copy validator (banned characters and words, Meta weight and body rules, premium framing).
- Brand font actually used (no fallback).
- Hook text in the first half second, captions present when there is speech, CTA or end card at the close, length inside the concept guide.

## Eye
1. **Frame one.** Would it stop a thumb? Face on screen, hook title up, no blink or half word.
2. **First three seconds.** Is the point of the ad clear, sound on and sound off?
3. **Read the timeline as a viewer.** Does the story make sense with the questions gone? Any line that only works with the question in front of it gets cut or swapped.
4. **Meaning.** Every testimonial line says what the client meant. No splice changes a claim.
5. **Rules.** No weight figures, body claims, health conditions, other businesses' results, or other clients. Anything borderline goes to Liam.
6. **Captions.** Match the audio word for word, break on natural phrases, never cover the face or mouth.
7. **Framing.** Face in the upper half on 9x16 (eye line around the upper third), never cropped at the chin or forehead, steady across cuts in both formats.
8. **Rhythm.** Something changes every three to five seconds. No jump cut lands mid-word. No double breaths or clipped consonants.
9. **Close.** The offer and CTA are clear, the end card is up long enough to read twice, the coach's line is not cut off.
10. **Length.** Inside the brief. If it runs long, the weakest beat goes, not a little from everywhere.
